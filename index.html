Privacy Policy
S&M Financing and Pawn Limited — Last updated: January 2025

S&M Financing and Pawn Limited ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the personal information of our customers in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Jamaica (2020).

1. Information We Collect

  • Full name, date of birth, TRN, and government-issued ID details
  • Residential address and contact information
  • Employment information including employer, job title, and monthly income
  • Financial information related to your loan request
  • Reference contact details provided by you
  • Documents including payslip, work ID, government ID, and proof of address
  • Your digital signature captured at the time of application
  • For JDF Soldiers: regimental number and Run Out Date

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To assess and process your loan application
  • To verify your identity and the accuracy of information provided
  • To communicate with you regarding your application status
  • To manage your loan account and repayment schedule
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations under Jamaican law
  • To contact you in the event of a missed or late payment

3. Delinquency and Public Disclosure

By submitting this application and accepting these terms, you acknowledge and agree that in the event of delinquency — defined as failure to repay the loan after the due date and after reasonable attempts to contact you have been made — S&M Financing and Pawn Limited reserves the right to use available information, including your name, photograph, and other identifying details, to publicly post information on the internet and social media platforms for the purpose of locating and recovering outstanding loan amounts. This measure will only be taken as a last resort after all other collection methods have been exhausted.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your personal information and uploaded documents are stored securely using industry-standard encryption. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secure servers with access controls that restrict access to authorised personnel only.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may only be shared where required by law, with authorised service providers under confidentiality agreements, or with credit reference agencies in the event of default.

6. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act of Jamaica (2020), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us at smfinancingandpawn@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

7. Retention of Data

We retain your personal information for a minimum of seven (7) years following the conclusion of the loan agreement.
