Privacy Policy
S&M Financing and Pawn Limited — Last updated: January 2025

S&M Financing and Pawn Limited ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the personal information of our customers in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Jamaica (2020). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you apply for a loan through our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a loan application, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name, date of birth, TRN, and government-issued ID details
  • Residential address and contact information (phone number and email address)
  • Employment information including employer name, job title, and monthly income
  • Financial information related to your loan request
  • Reference contact details provided by you
  • Documents uploaded including payslip, work ID, government-issued ID, and proof of address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the following purposes:

  • To assess and process your loan application
  • To verify your identity and the accuracy of information provided
  • To communicate with you regarding your application status
  • To manage your loan account and repayment schedule
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations under Jamaican law
  • To contact you in the event of a missed or late payment

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your personal information and uploaded documents are stored securely using industry-standard encryption. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secure servers with access controls that restrict access to authorised personnel only. We do not store your information on physical paper records once it has been entered into our system.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may only be shared in the following limited circumstances:

  • Where required by law or by order of a court of competent jurisdiction in Jamaica
  • With authorised agents or service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality agreements
  • With credit reference agencies or debt recovery services in the event of a default, where permitted by law

5. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act of Jamaica (2020), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal obligations
  • Withdraw consent for processing where consent is the basis for processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us directly at the details provided below.

6. Retention of Data

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Loan records are typically retained for a minimum of seven (7) years following the conclusion of the loan agreement.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our loan application platform does not use cookies or any tracking technologies to monitor your behaviour or collect data beyond what you voluntarily submit through the application form.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us at:
